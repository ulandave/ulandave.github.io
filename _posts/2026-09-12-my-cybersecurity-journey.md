## Introduction

My interest in cybersecurity developed from a curiosity about how computer systems work, how vulnerabilities are discovered, and how attackers can exploit weaknesses.

As a Computer Science student, cybersecurity has become one of the areas I am most interested in exploring.

## Learning by Doing

One of the biggest lessons from my cybersecurity journey has been the importance of hands-on practice.

Instead of only studying concepts theoretically, I have been using platforms such as:

* Hack The Box
* TryHackMe
* PortSwigger Academy
* EchoCTF

These platforms have allowed me to work with vulnerable systems and security scenarios in controlled environments.

## Areas of Interest

My current interests include:

### Offensive Security

I enjoy understanding how vulnerabilities can be discovered and exploited.

Areas I have explored include:

* Web application security
* SQL Injection
* SSRF
* Privilege escalation
* Linux security
* Network enumeration

### Defensive Security

I am also developing an interest in the defensive side of cybersecurity.

This includes:

* Security monitoring
* Network traffic analysis
* Incident investigation
* Log analysis
* Threat detection
* SOC operations

## Why SOC?

One area I am particularly interested in is Security Operations.

A SOC analyst needs to combine technical knowledge with investigation and problem-solving.

The process of receiving an alert, collecting evidence, identifying what happened, determining whether the activity is malicious, and recommending a response is particularly interesting to me.

## The Goal

The long-term goal is to become a well-rounded cybersecurity professional with strong skills in both offensive and defensive security.

I want to continue improving through:

* CTFs
* Security labs
* Personal projects
* Certifications
* Internships
* Security research
* Real-world experience
